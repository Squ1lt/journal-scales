Journal Day Data Scale Definitions

Version 1

Rate the day as a whole, from waking until the time of journaling.

Use whole numbers from 0–10.

Scales are absolute, not relative to what is normal for me. Do not recalibrate the scales as circumstances, age, health, or lifestyle change.

Day Satisfaction

Taking everything into account, how satisfied are you with today as a whole?

0 = Completely dissatisfied

5 = Neither satisfied nor dissatisfied overall

10 = Completely satisfied

Mood

Taking the day as a whole, how emotionally pleasant or unpleasant did you feel?

0 = Extremely unpleasant

5 = Neither pleasant nor unpleasant overall

10 = Extremely pleasant

Stress

Overall, how stressful did today feel?

0 = Not at all stressful

10 = Extremely stressful

Energy

How energetic, alert, and alive did you feel overall today?

0 = Completely depleted

10 = Extremely energetic, alert, and vigorous

Connection

How close and connected did you feel to other people today?

0 = Completely disconnected

10 = Extremely close and connected

Agency

To what extent did your actions today feel freely chosen and consistent with what you wanted or valued?

0 = Almost entirely pressured, imposed, or contrary to what I wanted or valued

10 = Almost entirely chosen and aligned with what I wanted or valued

Progress

How much meaningful forward progress did you make today on things that matter to you?

0 = No meaningful progress, or meaningful setback

10 = Extremely strong forward progress on things that matter to me

Meaning

To what extent did the things you did today feel worthwhile and meaningful?

0 = Not at all worthwhile or meaningful

10 = Completely worthwhile and deeply meaningful

Physical Wellbeing

Overall, how good did your body feel today, considering physical health, pain/discomfort, illness, and physical functioning?

0 = Extremely poor physical wellbeing

10 = Extremely good physical wellbeing
